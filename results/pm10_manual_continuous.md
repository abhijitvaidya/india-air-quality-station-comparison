# PM10 against CAMS PM10, by year

Continuous PM10: 22,557 valid station-months in 2016–2024 at 541 stations; the 538 with coordinates are compared. Manual PM10: 5,919 monthly values, 98 stations in 37 Uttar Pradesh cities, 2019–2025 (CAMS at the city centre for 19 cities without continuous stations). Each station is compared with itself in the same calendar month of other years. Intervals: 95%.
