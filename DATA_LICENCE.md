The code is under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`). The files in `data/derived/`, `results/`, `tables/` and `figures/` are
under different terms, set by their sources.

| Files | Source | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| `continuous_station_month_pm25.csv`, `continuous_station_month_pm10.csv` | CPCB continuous stations, through the XKDR Forum India air quality database; January to March and September to December 2025 from OpenAQ | CC BY 4.0 (XKDR Forum; OpenAQ) |
| `stations.csv` | XKDR Forum database; for 59 stations, OpenAQ and the CPCB station listing | CC BY 4.0 |
| `satellite_pm25_station_month_v6.csv`, `_v5.csv` | Atmospheric Composition Analysis Group, Washington University in St. Louis (V6.GL.03 and V5.GL.06) | CC BY 4.0 for V6.GL.03; V5.GL.06 as published by the group |
| `manual_station_month.csv` | Monthly reports of the Uttar Pradesh Pollution Control Board (UPPCB), January 2019 to December 2025 | UPPCB's terms: the reports may be downloaded; other use needs UPPCB's approval. These values are not covered by CC BY |
| `uppcb_reports.csv` | Addresses and checksums of those reports | CC BY 4.0 |
| `manual_city_centres.csv` | OpenStreetMap, through Nominatim | Open Database Licence |
| `results/`, `tables/`, `figures/` | This repository | CC BY 4.0, with the attributions above |

When you use these files, cite the paper (`CITATION.cff`) and the sources above.
